A regular post, with no writeup metadata — the header box above the content
stays hidden when those front-matter fields aren't set.

## Why write things down

Solving a challenge and never writing it up means relearning the same trick in
six months. Publishing forces the explanation to actually hold together.

## What to expect here

- CTF writeups, mostly web and pwn
- Notes on tooling I end up rewriting anyway
- Occasional deep dives when something surprises me
